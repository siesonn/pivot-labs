About Pivot Labs

Navigating Everyday Leadership

What this is

Pivot Labs is a set of free tools and visual guides for everyday leadership. Each tool walks you through a real situation, such as a conversation you need to have, a decision to make, or a problem that keeps coming back. It ends with something you can use: a short plan, a brief, or a picture you can share.

Leaders sometimes need to pivot. These tools are meant to help you decide what to change and what to do next.

How the tools and guides are made

Each tool and guide is built around a well-known leadership method. We use AI to help research and draft. A person reviews everything before it is published.

Credit and originality

Many leadership ideas come from researchers, practitioners, and teachers. Where a method has a known originator, we name them on the tool or guide, with sources. The drawings, structure, and wording on this site are our own. If you are an originator and want a credit changed, please write to us.

Your privacy

  • What you type into a tool stays on your screen. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere, and it disappears when you close or refresh the page.
  • We count visits with a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-ups.
