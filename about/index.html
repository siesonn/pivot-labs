About Pivot Labs

Navigating Everyday Leadership

What this is

Almost everyone who leads people ends up figuring it out as they go. The hard conversation, the full plate, the problem that keeps coming back: if that’s you, you’re in good company. Pivot Labs is a set of free tools and visual guides for those moments. Each tool walks you through a real situation and ends with something you can use: a short plan, a brief, or a picture you can share.

Sometimes a leader needs to pivot. That’s where the name comes from. These tools help you work out what to change and what to do next.

How the tools and guides are made

Every tool and guide starts from a well-known leadership method, because the best ideas are usually the ones plenty of leaders have already tested. AI helps with research and first drafts. A person reads and approves everything before it goes live.

Who we learned from

Most leadership ideas started with someone: a researcher, a practitioner, a teacher. When we know who, we name them on the tool or guide, with sources. The drawings, structure, and wording are our own. If you created a method we’ve used and want the credit changed, write to us.

Your privacy

  • What you type into a tool stays on your screen. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and it’s gone when you close or refresh the page.
  • We count visits with a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites.
  • No accounts, no sign-ups.
  • The optional feedback form is run by Google Forms. Whatever you type into it goes to Google and to us, so please leave out anything private. The email field is only if you’d like a reply.
