What do they need?Most asking to most telling →

Coach

Room to think it through and reach their own answer. You mostly ask.

Share

Your experience. You tell stories, not instructions, and they decide what fits.

Direct

Clear direction, because the work is new or something needs to change soon.

When they need room to think: four ways to coach

Find the goal

Work out what they want to grow toward, then pick one first step.

Build on what’s working

Look at times it went a little better, and build from there.

Weigh the options

Lay out the choices, what each gains and costs, and what matters most.

Turn it into action

Agree on a first step, a date, and how they’ll know it’s done.

When something seems off

Reconnect with the work

Say what you’ve noticed, ask what’s changed, and listen before you suggest anything.

Ask one question, then wait.

The approach fits the moment, not the person.
