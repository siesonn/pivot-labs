What others see,
and what you share

You pick five or six words. A few people pick words for you, from the same list of strengths.

Four boxes

Known to you
Not known to you
Known to others
Not known to others

Open

You and others picked these. What people already see in you.

Blind spot

Others picked these. You didn’t. A strength you may not count.

Hidden

You picked these. Others didn’t, yet. A strength people haven’t seen much.

Unknown

No one picked these this time. That doesn’t mean they’re missing.

Two ways to grow the Open box

Ask

Ask people what they see. Your Blind spot gets smaller.

“When have you seen me be calm?”

Share

Tell people how you work. Your Hidden box gets smaller.

“Bring me the numbers work.”

It’s a conversation, not a rating.

Pick on your own, then talk.
