Two questions about one task point to four ways to support someone.

Guide

Willing, still building skill

Show how it is done, let them try a piece, and give feedback soon after.

Example: a teammate who asked to run the weekly meeting for the first time.

Stretch

Skilled and willing

Hand over ownership. Agree on the outcome and the check-ins.

Example: a teammate who leads meetings well and wants to lead the quarterly review.

Direct

Building skill, hesitant

Give clear steps and a short first goal. Check in often.

Example: a new hire asked to build a report in a tool they have not used.

Re-engage

Skilled, hesitant

Find out what is in the way before changing anything.

Example: an experienced teammate who has gone quiet on a report they used to own.

Then ask: what is behind the hesitation?

Direct and Re-engage both start there, and the cause changes the first step.

  • Full workload
  • Not sure they can
  • Unclear why it matters
  • Not interested

If you do not know, ask and listen before suggesting changes.

Idea: Max Landsberg, The Tao of Coaching (1996). Pivot Labs renamed two of the four approaches and added the question about hesitation. The drawing and wording are Pivot Labs’ own.
