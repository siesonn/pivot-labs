Who decides what

Agree on it before you hand it off.

More up to themMore with you

They decide

They make the call and keep going.

Often fits
  • How to do the work
  • Day-to-day priorities

They decide, then tell you

They make the call, then let you know soon after.

Often fits
  • Asking others for help
  • Updates to senior leaders, clients, or other teams

Check with you first

They come to you before acting, with what they’d suggest.

Often fits
  • Spending money
  • Changing the deadline
  • Changing what done looks like

Start where you’re both comfortable.

Loosen it as it goes well. The level fits the decision, not the person.

Idea: William Oncken, Jr. and Donald L. Wass, “Management Time: Who’s Got the Monkey?” (1974). Pivot Labs uses three levels. The drawing and wording are Pivot Labs’ own.
