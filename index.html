What do you need help with?

Start with what you’re working through, then choose the tool that fits.

Prioritize your time

Decide what gets attention now and what can wait.

  • Eisenhower Matrix

    Work through your priorities. Add your tasks, consider urgency and importance, and decide what to do, schedule, delegate, or reconsider.

    Open the Eisenhower Matrix

Set expectations and run the team

Make roles, handoffs, and team routines clear.

  • Delegation Planner

    Plan one handoff. Agree on what done looks like, who decides what, and when you’ll check in, then print a brief to review together.

    Open the Delegation Planner

Have the conversation

Prepare what to say, what to ask, and how to follow up.

  • Feedback Conversation Planner

    Prepare one feedback conversation. Describe what happened, get a suggested approach, and print a plan to bring with you.

    Open the Feedback Conversation Planner

Develop your people

Choose how to coach, stretch, or support each person.

  • Team Development Map

    Plan how to support someone on a specific task. Add a person and a task, consider their skill and willingness, and decide whether to guide, stretch, re-engage, or direct.

    Open the Team Development Map
