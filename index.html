From a framework to a plan

You’ve probably heard of a few leadership frameworks in your career, even if you can’t recall the name or how it works. Here are many of them in one place, and most are interactive tools you fill in with your own work.

  1. 1

    Start with what’s going on

    Pick the tile below that sounds closest, like “I have too much on my plate.”

  2. 2

    Answer a question or two

    Most tiles ask first, then suggest a tool and tell you why it fits.

  3. 3

    Leave with a plan

    Fill it in, then print it or save it as a PDF before you close the page.
