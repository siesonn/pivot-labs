90-Day Leader Plan

How will you spend the next 90 days?

New role, new team, or a team in the middle of a change? Plan the next three months in three parts: listen and learn, make sense of it together, then act and check in. It takes about 10 minutes, and you leave with a plan you can print.

    About this tool

    The 90-Day Leader Plan helps you plan your first three months in a new role, with a new team, or through a big change. Splitting the time into three 30-day parts is common practice. The ideas of learning before you act, matching your approach to the situation, and agreeing early with your manager on what success looks like come from Michael D. Watkins’ book The First 90 Days. The ideas of saying what stops, closing the loop, and keeping steady one-on-ones come from Michaela Schuler’s infographic “How to Rebuild Team Culture in the First 90 Days.” The structure and wording are our own.

    Sources: Watkins, M. D. (2003; updated 2013). The First 90 Days: Proven Strategies for Getting Up to Speed Faster and Smarter. Harvard Business Review Press. Schuler, M. How to Rebuild Team Culture in the First 90 Days (infographic, LinkedIn).
