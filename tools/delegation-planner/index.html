    About this tool

    The Delegation Planner helps you plan one handoff: what done looks like, who decides what, how you’ll both know it’s on track, and when you’ll check in. Its decision levels are a simpler version of the five degrees of initiative that William Oncken, Jr. and Donald L. Wass describe in “Management Time: Who’s Got the Monkey?” They write that every handoff needs an agreed level of initiative and a set time for the next check-in. This Pivot Labs version uses three levels and adds questions about readiness, measures, and how you’ll step back.

    Source: Oncken, W., Jr., & Wass, D. L. (1974). Management time: Who’s got the monkey? Harvard Business Review.
