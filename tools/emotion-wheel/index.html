    About this tool

    The Emotion Wheel helps you put a word to what you’re feeling, think about what might be behind it, and choose a next step. It follows the idea behind feeling wheels: a few basic feelings, with more exact words under each. Well-known versions include Robert Plutchik’s wheel of emotions (1980), Gloria Willcox’s Feeling Wheel (1982), and the Junto Institute’s Emotion & Feeling Wheel. Research on how people group emotion words, such as the work of Shaver and colleagues (1987), points to a similar shape. The wheel, the word list, and the questions here are our own. This is a reflection tool, not therapy or a diagnosis.
