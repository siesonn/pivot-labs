After you name it

What is your feeling telling you?

A feeling often points to something you need or care about. Use these as things to consider, then choose one next move.

FeelingIt often points toA next move
  • Joy

    Something is working or matters to you.

    Notice what made it happen, and say so to the people involved.

  • Connection

    Trust or belonging is building.

    Thank someone specifically, and protect what is building it.

  • Surprise

    A gap between what you expected and what happened.

    Get the facts and ask a question before you react.

  • Fear

    Uncertainty, or something you care about feels at risk.

    Name the one thing you don’t know, then ask for it or ask for support.

  • Sadness

    A loss, or a hope that didn’t happen.

    Name what you hoped for, then decide what is still in your hands.

  • Anger

    A standard, a boundary, or a need wasn’t respected.

    Pause before you respond. Then name the standard and make a clear request.

Not sure which feeling you have? Start with the Emotion Wheel at pivotlabsleadership.com.

For reflection, not a diagnosis. If a feeling stays heavy, talk with someone you trust or a professional. The wording is Pivot Labs’ own.
