Executive Ask Builder

What decision do you need?

Need a decision from your manager or senior leaders? Say what you’re asking for, give just enough context, get ready for their concerns, and leave with a one-page brief.

    About this tool

    The Executive Ask Builder helps you prepare one ask: the decision you need, just enough context, the concerns you’re likely to hear, and how you’ll close. It is adapted from Cicely Simpson’s infographic on presenting to senior leadership. The structure and wording are our own.

    Source: Simpson, C. How to Present to Senior Leadership as a Leader (infographic, LinkedIn).
