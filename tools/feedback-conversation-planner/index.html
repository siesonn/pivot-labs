    About this tool

    The Feedback Conversation Planner helps you prepare one feedback conversation: what to say, what to ask, and how to follow up. Your answers suggest one of six approaches, and you can choose another. Every approach describes actions and their effect, not a person’s character.

    Describing when and where something happened, what the person did, and its effect comes from the Situation-Behavior-Impact™ (SBI™) model by the Center for Creative Leadership. Opening with context and closing with an agreed next step come from Anna Carroll’s COIN conversation model. Look ahead draws on feedforward, Marshall Goldsmith’s term for offering ideas for the future instead of reviewing the past. The two approaches for a repeated issue are inspired by Jonathan Raymond’s Accountability Dial®, which raises an issue in gradual steps; their structure and wording are our own. The reminder to separate what you saw from your guess about why draws on Chris Argyris’s ladder of inference. The “Before you talk” checklist is adapted from infographics by Amy Gibson and Cicely Simpson.
