All Tools

Already know what you need? Every tool, grouped by what you’re working on. Free, no account, and each one runs in your browser.

Prioritize your time

  • Eisenhower MatrixSort your tasks by urgency and importance.
  • Time Method PickerFind one time method to try for a week.

Set expectations and run the team

  • Delegation PlannerPlan one handoff, from the outcome to check-ins.
  • Role Clarity BuilderMake one role clear: what it runs, what it shares, and who decides what.

Have the conversation

  • Feedback Conversation PlannerPrepare one feedback conversation.
  • Executive Ask BuilderPrepare to ask your manager or senior leaders for a decision.

Develop your people

  • Team Development MapDecide how to support someone on a task.
  • Coaching Approach PickerChoose how to coach someone, and plan the conversation.
  • Readiness Matrix BuilderMap what ready looks like in a role, stage by stage.

Decide, solve and plan ahead

  • Root Cause FinderFind the cause of a problem and plan the fix.
  • Decision Approach PickerChoose how to decide, and record what you decided and why.

Lead change and build trust

  • 90-Day Leader PlanPlan your first three months in a new role, or through a big change.

Know yourself as a leader

  • Emotion WheelName what you’re feeling and decide what to do next.
  • Managing or Leading CheckSee where your work runs through you and what to try next.
  • Johari WindowSee how you see yourself, and how others see you.
