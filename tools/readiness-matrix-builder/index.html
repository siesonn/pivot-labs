Readiness Matrix Builder

What does ready look like in this role?

Map how someone grows in a role, from their first weeks to doing it at a high level. You’ll describe what each stage looks like and how you’d know someone is there. It’s for coaching and development conversations, not for grading anyone.

i
This one takes a while, about an hour. Nothing is saved, so keep this tab open until you’ve printed your matrix or saved it as a PDF.

    About this tool

    This builder comes from Pivot Labs’ own readiness framework. It describes how someone grows in a role, from their first weeks to doing the work at a high level, based on behavior you can see.

    It draws on the idea of Return on Expectations from Donald and Jim Kirkpatrick: judge development by whether it delivers what people expected, not only by a dollar figure. Return on Expectations is a service mark of Kirkpatrick Partners. This tool isn’t connected to or endorsed by them.
