    About this tool

    The Role Clarity Builder helps you make one role clear: what it runs, what it shares with others, what belongs to someone else, who it works with, and who decides what. It is inspired by Cicely Simpson’s Role Clarity Document infographic. The structure and wording are our own. Its decision levels are a simpler version of the degrees of initiative that William Oncken, Jr. and Donald L. Wass describe in “Management Time: Who’s Got the Monkey?” Research on role ambiguity, beginning with Kahn and colleagues (1964) and Rizzo, House, and Lirtzman (1970), links unclear expectations to strain at work.

    Sources: Simpson, C. Role Clarity Document (infographic, LinkedIn). Oncken, W., Jr., & Wass, D. L. (1974). Management time: Who’s got the monkey? Harvard Business Review. Kahn, R. L., Wolfe, D. M., Quinn, R. P., Snoek, J. D., & Rosenthal, R. A. (1964). Organizational stress: Studies in role conflict and ambiguity. Wiley. Rizzo, J. R., House, R. J., & Lirtzman, S. I. (1970). Role conflict and ambiguity in complex organizations. Administrative Science Quarterly, 15(2), 150-163.
