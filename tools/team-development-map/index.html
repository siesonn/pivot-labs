About this tool

The Team Development Map is based on the Skill/Will Matrix that Max Landsberg describes in The Tao of Coaching (1996). Landsberg matches a coaching approach to a person’s skill and will for a specific task. This Pivot Labs version renames two of his approaches, Delegate and Excite, as Stretch and Re-engage. It adds guided questions about what you’ve seen and what might be behind hesitation.

Source: Landsberg, M. (1996). The Tao of Coaching. HarperCollins.

A quick check-in

Choose how to support them on this task.

How ready is to do this task well today?

Think about work you’ve seen them do, not their title, how long they’ve been here, or how confident they sound.

How willing is to take this on right now?

Think about this task and this moment. Willingness can shift with workload, interest, and confidence.

What might be behind the hesitation?

Choose the closest fit. If you’re guessing, choose “I don’t know.”
